## Scope widened (added by the board, 2026-10-14)

The owner wants **any business that runs from a computer**, not mostly
software. The one hard rule: **no inventory we hold or manufacture.**

Explicitly in scope, and at least half of every new idea batch must come
from this list until the board says otherwise:
- **Print-on-demand** stores (Printful / Printify fulfilment on Etsy or a
  Shopify Starter store): a niche, a design system, a product line. The
  supplier prints and ships; we never touch stock.
- **Digital products:** templates (Notion, Figma, spreadsheets, Canva),
  printables, presets, stock assets, courses and guides.
- **Content businesses:** newsletters, niche content or comparison sites
  earning from affiliate links, sponsorships or a paid tier.
- Marketplaces and directories with no physical goods.

Still out: holding stock, manufacturing, dropshipping that needs us to handle
returns or customer goods, offline work, client services, regulated products.

Money rules are unchanged: spend ≤ €25/month and only after paid pre-orders.
Opening a store, a marketplace seller account or a payment account is an
owner step: raise one board question when an idea is ready for it. Prefer
platforms that fit the budget (Etsy, Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Shopify Starter)
over Shopify Basic. Validate POD and digital ideas the same way as software:
evidence that buyers already pay (best-seller lists, review counts, competitor
sales estimates), a mock-up or listing page, then a signal before building.
